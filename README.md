# 图像对比实验 · Image comparison study

两张压缩过的照片，哪一张更接近原图？这是一项关于图像压缩质量的研究，需要大家用眼睛来判断。
*Two compressed versions of a photo: which one is closer to the original? A study on image compression quality.*

## 参加实验 · Take part

- 打开 <https://fengyurenpingsheng.github.io/image-comparison-study/>，填一个名字或邮箱，跟着页面提示做就行，约 15 分钟。
  *Open <https://fengyurenpingsheng.github.io/image-comparison-study/?lang=en>, type a name or e-mail and follow the instructions; about 15 minutes.*
- 第一次参加可以先看 **[图文教程 / illustrated guide](https://fengyurenpingsheng.github.io/image-comparison-study/guide.html)**。
- 需要：带键盘的电脑，屏幕至少 1600×900 像素（手机和平板不行）。
  *You need a computer with a keyboard and a screen of at least 1600×900 pixels (no phones or tablets).*
- 自愿参加：名字或邮箱只用来区分结果，不会公开；随时可以退出，进度会保存。
  *Voluntary: the name or e-mail only tells the results apart and is not made public; you can stop at any time and your progress is saved.*
