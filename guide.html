图像对比实验 · 图文教程

整个实验大约 30 分钟。下面按顺序截图说明每一步，跟着做就行。

Image comparison study · illustrated guide

The whole study takes about 30 minutes. Each step is shown below with a screenshot.

开始前准备好：一台电脑（笔记本或台式机，带键盘）；屏幕至少 1600×900 像素；Chrome、Edge、Firefox 或 Safari 浏览器； 一个安静、光线正常的地方；大约 30 分钟。手机和平板不行。
You need: a computer (laptop or desktop with a keyboard); a screen of at least 1600×900 pixels; Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari; a quiet place with normal light; about 30 minutes. Phones and tablets will not work.
1

打开组织者发给你的链接

Open the link the organizer sent you

链接里已经带了你的编号（例如 P05），页面上会显示出来。每个人的编号不一样，请只用自己的链接。点「开始」。

The link already contains your code (for example P05), which the page shows. Everyone has a different code; please use only your own link. Click "Start".

2

阅读参与说明，勾选两项，点「同意并继续」

Read the information, tick both boxes, click "Agree and continue"

接下来有几个关于你的问题（年龄段、视力等），都可以不答，直接点「下一步」。

A few optional questions follow (age group, eyesight, ...); you can skip them all and click "Next".

3

检查屏幕：两项都变绿再继续

Screen check: continue when both items are green

两项都是绿色：可以点「下一步」。
Both green: click "Next".
「缩放比例」是红的：需要调浏览器缩放。
"Scaling" red: change the browser zoom.
「缩放比例」是红的怎么办？很多 Windows 笔记本把显示缩放设成了 125% 或 150%。按住 Ctrl 再按 -， 把浏览器缩放调到 80%（125% 的电脑）或 67%（150% 的电脑），这一项就会变绿。Mac 上按 ⌘ 和 - / +。
「窗口大小」是红的怎么办？点「全屏」。还是红的，说明这块屏幕太小，请换一台电脑或接一个外接显示器。
"Scaling" is red? Many Windows laptops use display scaling of 125% or 150%. Hold Ctrl and press - to set the browser zoom to 80% (125% laptops) or 67% (150% laptops) and the item turns green. On a Mac use ⌘ with - / +.
"Window size" is red? Click "Full screen". If it stays red, the screen is too small: please use another computer or an external monitor.
4

看懂怎么做，并试按三个键

Learn the task and try the three keys

每题左右各有一张压缩图。按住空格，两边都换成原图；松开，回到两张压缩图。选出更接近原图的那张： F 左边，J 右边。三个键都试过（变绿）以后，点「开始练习」。

Each trial shows two compressed images. Hold Space and both become the original; release to see the compressed ones again. Pick the one closer to the original: F left, J right. Once all three keys are green, click "Start practice".

5

练习 6 题（会告诉你对不对）

6 practice trials (with feedback)

实验时页面会变成全屏、灰色背景。下面左图是平时看到的样子；右图是按住空格时，两边都变成了原图。

The study runs full screen on a grey background. Left: what you normally see. Right: while you hold Space, both sides show the original.

平时：两张压缩图
normally: two compressed images
按住空格：两边都是原图
holding Space: both show the original
练习题选完会提示对错（正式题不提示）
practice trials tell you whether you were right (the main part does not)
练完可以再练一遍，或者开始正式实验
practise again, or start the main part
6

正式实验：约 25 分钟，中间有 4 次休息

Main part: about 25 minutes, with 4 breaks

底部有进度条。差别常常很小，看不出来时凭第一感觉选一个，不用纠结，一般每题 3–6 秒。休息时想歇多久都可以。

A progress bar is at the bottom. Differences are often small; if you cannot tell, go with your first impression (usually 3–6 seconds per trial). Rest as long as you like during the breaks.

7

提交结果

Submit the result

点「提交结果」，浏览器会下载一个以 image_study_ 开头的文件（一般在「下载」文件夹里）。请按页面上的提示把这个文件发给组织者 （如果页面显示"已自动提交"，组织者已经收到了）。

Click "Submit the result". The browser downloads a file whose name starts with image_study_ (usually in your Downloads folder). Please send this file to the organizer as the page says (if the page says it was sent automatically, the organizer already has it).

常见问题

Questions

中途要离开怎么办？
直接关掉网页就行，每答一题都会自动保存。之后用同一台电脑、同一个浏览器打开同一个链接，点「继续上次」。
按 F、J 没反应？
先用鼠标点一下页面，再把输入法切换成英文。
图片加载很慢？
稍等一下，后面的题会提前加载。网速实在太慢，可以在 GitHub 仓库页面点 Code → Download ZIP（约 180 MB），解压后双击 index.html，选自己的编号来做。
两张图看起来一样？
很正常，很多题差别都很小。凭第一感觉选一个就行。
做完忘了发文件？
用同一台电脑、同一个浏览器打开同一个链接，点「查看结果 / 再次下载」。
有其他问题？
请联系发给你链接的组织者。
I need to stop halfway.
Just close the page; every answer is saved. Later, open the same link on the same computer and browser and click "Continue".
F and J do nothing.
Click once on the page, then switch your keyboard input to English.
Images load slowly.
Wait a moment; later trials are loaded ahead. On a very slow connection, download the repository as a ZIP from GitHub (Code → Download ZIP, about 180 MB), unzip it, double-click index.html and choose your code.
The two images look the same.
That is normal; many differences are small. Go with your first impression.
I finished but forgot to send the file.
Open the same link on the same computer and browser and click "See result / download again".
Other questions?
Please contact the organizer who sent you the link.
